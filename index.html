📘 Math Formula

➕ Arithmetic

Addition: a + b

Subtraction: a − b

Multiplication: a × b

Division: a ÷ b

Percentage = (part ÷ whole) × 100

Percentage Increase = [(new − old) ÷ old] × 100

Percentage Decrease = [(old − new) ÷ old] × 100

Average = sum ÷ total number

Weighted Average = Σ(wx) ÷ Σw

Ratio a : b = a/b

Proportion a/b = c/d ⇒ ad = bc

Speed = distance ÷ time

Distance = speed × time

Time = distance ÷ speed

Simple Interest = (P × R × T) ÷ 100

Compound Interest = P(1 + R/100)n − P

LCM × HCF = product of two numbers

📊 Algebra

ax + b = 0

x = −b ÷ a

ax² + bx + c = 0

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²

(a + b)³ = a³ + b³ + 3ab(a + b)

(a − b)³ = a³ − b³ − 3ab(a − b)

a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)

a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²)

a³ − b³ = (a − b)(a² + ab + b²)

Quadratic Formula: x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a

Discriminant = b² − 4ac

Linear Equation (2 variables): ax + by + c = 0

📐 Geometry

Area of Square = side²

Perimeter of Square = 4 × side

Diagonal of Square = side√2

Area of Rectangle = l × w

Perimeter of Rectangle = 2(l + w)

Diagonal of Rectangle = √(l² + w²)

Area of Triangle = ½ × base × height

Heron’s Formula = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]

s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2

Area of Circle = πr²

Circumference of Circle = 2πr

Area of annulus (ring) = π(R² − r²)

Area of sector = (θ/360) × πr²

Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr

Pythagoras Theorem: c² = a² + b²

📦 Mensuration

Volume of Cube = a³

Surface Area of Cube = 6a²

Volume of Cuboid = l × w × h

Total Surface Area of Cuboid = 2(lw + lh + wh)

Volume of Cylinder = πr²h

Curved Surface Area of Cylinder = 2πrh

Volume of Cone = ⅓πr²h

Slant Height of Cone = √(r² + h²)

Volume of Sphere = 4/3πr³

Surface Area of Sphere = 4πr²

Volume of Hemisphere = 2/3πr³

📐 Trigonometry

sinθ = opposite / hypotenuse

cosθ = adjacent / hypotenuse

tanθ = opposite / adjacent

cosecθ = 1/sinθ

secθ = 1/cosθ

cotθ = 1/tanθ

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

1 + tan²θ = sec²θ

1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ

sin(90° − θ) = cosθ

tan(45°) = 1

📈 Statistics

Mean = Σx ÷ n

Median = middle value

Mode = most frequent value

Range = max − min

Variance = Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ n

Standard Deviation = √Variance

Probability = favourable ÷ total outcomes

P(event) + P(not event) = 1

🔢 Number System

Even Number = 2n

Odd Number = 2n + 1

Prime Number: divisible by 1 and itself

Composite Number: more than two factors

Natural Numbers = {1, 2, 3, ...}

Whole Numbers = {0, 1, 2, 3, ...}

Integers = {..., −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, ...}

Rational Number = p/q, q ≠ 0

Irrational Number ≠ p/q

Absolute Value = |x|

📘 Logarithms

logₐ1 = 0

logₐa = 1

log(ab) = loga + logb

log(a/b) = loga − logb

log(aⁿ) = n loga

Change of Base: logₐb = log b / log a

🧩 Sets

Union: A ∪ B

Intersection: A ∩ B

Difference: A − B

n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B)

Complement of A = U − A

Empty Set = ∅

📐 Matrices

Order of Matrix = rows × columns

Addition: A + B (same order)

Multiplication: AB (columns of A = rows of B)

Identity Matrix = I

Determinant of 2×2 Matrix: |a b| |c d| = ad − bc

Inverse of 2×2 Matrix: A⁻¹ = 1/det(A) × | d −b | | −c a |

📍 Coordinate Geometry

Distance Formula = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]

Midpoint Formula = ((x₁ + x₂)/2 , (y₁ + y₂)/2)

Slope (m) = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)

Equation of Line: y = mx + c

Area of Triangle = ½|x₁(y₂−y₃)+x₂(y₃−y₁)+x₃(y₁−y₂)|

🔁 Sequences & Series

Arithmetic Progression (AP): a, a+d, a+2d, ...

nᵗʰ term of AP = a + (n−1)d

Sum of AP = n/2 [2a + (n−1)d]

Geometric Progression (GP): a, ar, ar², ...

nᵗʰ term of GP = arⁿ⁻¹

Sum of GP = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1)

📉 Calculus

Derivative: d/dx (xⁿ) = nxⁿ⁻¹

d/dx (sin x) = cos x

d/dx (cos x) = −sin x

d/dx (eˣ) = eˣ

Integration: ∫xⁿ dx = xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1) + C

∫sin x dx = −cos x + C

∫cos x dx = sin x + C

Fundamental Theorem: ∫ₐᵇ f′(x) dx = f(b) − f(a)

🎲 Permutation & Combination

Factorial: n! = n(n−1)(n−2)...1

Permutation: nPr = n!/(n−r)!

Combination: nCr = n!/[r!(n−r)!]

nCr = nC(n−r)
